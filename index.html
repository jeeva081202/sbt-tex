Our story

From the looms of Kasi Nagar

S.Bakkiyalaxmi Tex weaves cotton sarees in T.Subbulapuram, near Aundipatti in Theni district. Each saree is woven by hand, one thread at a time, in the checks, buttas and rich borders that Tamil homes have worn for generations.

We sell straight from our looms to you, so you get an honest price and a saree made to be worn and loved.
